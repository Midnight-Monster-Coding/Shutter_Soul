About Shutter Soul

Welcome to Shutter Soul - where photography meets competition! This is a daily photo challenge platform where photographers showcase their skills and compete for monthly glory.

Upload one original photo per day, get AI-rated based on composition, technical quality, and creativity, and climb the rankings to earn prestigious medals.

How It Works

  • One Photo Per Day: Upload one original photo captured by you daily (max 10MB)
  • AI Rating System: Each photo receives a base score from 0-77 from our AI
  • Engage & Earn: You can like and comment on other users' posts. When others like your photos, it adds extra points to your score!
  • Community Chat: Connect directly with the community—here you can also chat with other users.
  • Monthly Competition: Your daily scores and bonus likes accumulate throughout the month
  • Real-Time Rankings: Watch your position change as you and others upload photos
  • Win Medals: Top 3 photographers at month-end receive Gold, Silver, or Bronze medals

Rating System

Our AI analyzes your photos based on professional photography standards, and your score gets boosted by community likes:

Composition & Balance 25 points
Technical Quality 20 points
Color & Lighting 17 points
Originality & Diversity 15 points
Base Maximum AI Score 77 points
Community Likes + Extra Bonus Points

Photo Requirements

  • It is highly recommended that you upload your own captured photos. No downloaded photos are accepted here.
  • No AI-generated images
  • No duplicate or highly similar photos
  • Supported formats: JPG, PNG, WebP
  • Maximum file size: 10MB
  • Photos are actively verified using AI detection systems

Monthly Reset

At the end of each month:

  • Winners are declared and medals are awarded
  • Scores reset to zero for everyone
  • A fresh competition begins on the 1st
  • Medals remain in your achievements forever

Tips for Better Scores

  • Use the Rule of Thirds for composition
  • Ensure good lighting and proper exposure
  • Keep your photos sharp and well-focused
  • Experiment with different angles and perspectives
  • Be active in the community—more visibility often brings more likes
